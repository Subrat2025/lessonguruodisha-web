🎯 Activity 1: Counting Stones in Pairs

⏱ Time: 10 minutes  |  👥 Group: Pair work
📦 Materials: 20 small stones / bottle caps per pair
🎓 Skill Focus: Counting, Number Recognition

Steps:

  1. Pair students up and distribute 20 stones to each pair.
  2. Teacher calls out a number (e.g., "7"). Pairs quickly count out 7 stones.
  3. Then "add 3 more" — pairs solve 7 + 3 by stacking.
  4. First pair to show the right total wins a star sticker.
  5. Repeat with different numbers, scaling difficulty.

🎯 Activity 2: Number Hunt Around the Classroom

⏱ Time: 8 minutes  |  👥 Group: Individual
📦 Materials: Worksheet, pencil
🎓 Skill Focus: Observation, Counting

Steps:

  1. Give each student a worksheet listing 5 items (chairs, windows, etc.)
  2. Students walk around and count each item.
  3. Back at desks, they write totals and add any 2 to find the sum.
  4. Share answers as a class for verification.
