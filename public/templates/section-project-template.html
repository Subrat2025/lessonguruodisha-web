📁 Project 1: My Family Number Chart

🎯 Objective: Apply addition / counting in real life by collecting family data.
⏱ Duration: 1 week (homework + class share)
👥 Type: Individual
📦 Materials needed: Chart paper, sketch pens, ruler, family photos (optional)
📋 Steps to Follow:
  1. Count the number of members in your family.
  2. Write each member's name and age on a chart.
  3. Add up the ages of all members → total family age.
  4. Decorate the chart with family photos / drawings.
  5. Present in class on Friday.
🏆 Evaluation Rubric:

PresentationClear & confidentHesitantUnable to explain
